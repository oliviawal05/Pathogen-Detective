🔬 A Detective's Guide to Deduction 🕵️

The aim of the game is to identify the most likely pathogen using the clinical information and answers to your questions.

🧩 This is a deduction game, not a test of whether a pathogen could ever display a particular characteristic in every possible circumstance.

🦠 Answers are based on the typical characteristics of each pathogen. Some characteristics will vary between strains, clinical circumstances or patients.

🔍 Use the clues together to narrow down your suspects and choose the pathogen that best fits the case!

Think like a microbiologist, investigate like a detective!

📋 How to Play

1. Select a clinical case to begin your investigation.

2. Read the patient's background, symptoms, clinical updates and laboratory findings.

3. Select questions from the available categories. Each question will provide a YES or NO answer.

4. Use the answers to eliminate unlikely pathogens from your suspect list.

5. When you think you have identified the pathogen, submit your diagnosis.

6. Once you correctly identify the pathogen, read the case explanation to reinforce your microbiology knowledge.

7. Continue with another case or select End Game to view your final results.

🏆 How Scoring Works 🏆

⭐ Each clinical case starts with 100 points.

❓ Each question asked deducts 5 points.

❌ Each incorrect diagnosis deducts 10 points.

✅ Your remaining points are awarded when you correctly identify the pathogen.

📊 Scores from successfully completed cases are added together to calculate your overall score.

There are 8 clinical cases, giving you a maximum possible score of 800 points.

Think carefully, use your clues and aim for the highest score!
